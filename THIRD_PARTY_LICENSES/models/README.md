# The model

DeepFilterNet2's weights are part of this archive, in
`bin/_internal/weights/deepfilternet2/deepfilternet2.safetensors` (9 MB). They
are [DeepFilterNet2](https://github.com/Rikorose/DeepFilterNet) by Hendrik
Schröter, released under the MIT licence ([LICENSE-MIT.txt](LICENSE-MIT.txt))
or the Apache License 2.0 ([LICENSE-APACHE.txt](LICENSE-APACHE.txt)), at your
option, as converted to SafeTensors by the audio.cpp project, from whose
source tree, at the commit `bin/_internal/native.json` names, the build copies
them, checked against the sha256 `spec.BUNDLED_SHA256` pins (in the add-on's
source, `deepfilternet_addon/spec.py`).
